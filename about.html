About BleauWeb

A small maker of small apps for Apple platforms.

Our mission is simple: to create apps that solve problems while remaining calm, collected, and to the point.

Calm

Software has learned to shout: notifications, streaks, badges, red dots, one more nudge about what you have not done yet. Our apps don't. Two of them never send a notification at all, and the one that can (a heads-up before a meal) keeps it off until you ask. They are built neurodivergent-friendly first, for people who find interruption expensive. An app should show you what you came for when you open it, and be silent when you don't.

Collected

Everything in its place, and your things in yours. There are no accounts to create and no servers of ours holding your data — what you make lives in your own Apple services and syncs through your own private iCloud, which we cannot read. Every app keeps its documents on this site: what it is, how to get help, and a privacy policy short enough to actually read, because there is almost nothing to say.

To the point

Each app solves one problem and stops. Screens stay predictable, words stay plain, every setting explains exactly what it does, and accessibility — VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, honest tap targets, Reduce Motion — is part of the build, not an afterthought. When a feature would make an app louder or vaguer, it stays out.

One app, one job

We made a conscious decision not to roll everything into a single application, even though these apps share a philosophy and could share a shell. A do-everything app has to keep changing, and every change lands on someone who had finally gotten comfortable. Keeping each app hyperfocused on one job means it can stay small, stay familiar, and stay finished. If you only need the planner, the planner is all you ever have to learn.

Who

BleauWeb is small on purpose. The person who shapes the apps is the person who answers the support inbox, so write about anything: support@bleauweb.org.
