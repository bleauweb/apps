Site Privacy

Effective 20 August 2026 · Covers bleauweb.org and its subdomains, including this site. Each app has its own policy, linked from its pages.

These pages set no cookies, run no analytics scripts, show no ads, and contain no trackers. What follows is the honest remainder: what serving a website involves at all.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives the standard technical details of a request — your IP address, browser type, and the page asked for — and handles them under GitHub's privacy statement. We never see those logs.

Cloudflare, and the numbers we do see

Traffic to bleauweb.org routes through Cloudflare, which provides DNS and protects the site the way it does for a large part of the web. Cloudflare processes requests to do that job, under Cloudflare's privacy policy, and shows us aggregate numbers about the site itself: roughly how many visits, from which countries, to which pages. Those numbers are counts, not people. They involve no cookies we control, and nothing in them identifies you to us — we could not look you up in them if we tried.

Email

If you write to support@bleauweb.org, we receive your address and whatever you chose to say, and we use them to reply. Nothing more is done with either.

The apps

The apps documented here are separate from this website and each has its own one-page policy: NeuRoutine, Wiederfind, Meals. The short version of all three: the apps collect nothing.

Changes

If how the site is served ever changes in a way that matters here, this page will change with it, and the date at the top will move.
