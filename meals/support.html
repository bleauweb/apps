Questions, answered

Why does every meal need an exact time?

Because "sometime this evening" is a decision you have to keep re-making all evening. 17:30 is settled. Meal blocks are real events in your own Calendar at fixed times, every day, and they hold still unless you move them. Predictability is the feature.

What do texture and temperature have to do with a recipe?

On a difficult day, whether food happens can depend less on what a dish contains and more on what it is like to eat: crunchy or soft, hot or cold, mixed together or kept separate. Recipes in Meals record that, so you can pick by how much you can handle right now.

What is the safe-food roster?

The short list of foods that always work for you. It sits one tap away, so on the worst day there is no browsing, no deciding, and no recipe to follow. Just the things you already know you can eat.

Does Meals count calories or judge what I ate?

No. There are no calorie targets, no weight goals, no streaks, and no score of any kind. Logging a meal records that it happened and, only if you switched Apple Health on, writes the nutrition there. The app keeps no opinion about any of it.

Does Meals send notifications?

Only if you ask it to. Meal warnings give you a heads-up a few minutes before a block starts, because knowing a transition is coming makes it easier. They are off by default, and the app never asks for notification permission until you switch the toggle on. There are no other notifications of any kind.

How does the grocery list work?

It leaves the app as reminders in a Groceries list, grouped by aisle in the order you walk the shop. Tick items off in Meals or in Apple's Reminders; they are the same list.

Where is my data stored?

Meal blocks live in your Calendar and groceries in your Reminders, so they sync between your devices through your own iCloud, like any calendar. Recipes, textures, the safe-food roster and meal status stay on your device. Nutrition goes to Apple Health only if you turned that on. There is no Meals server and no account.

Why does the app open in dark mode?

Because dinner time is often dim-kitchen time, and an app that opens bright white at 17:30 is a flash to the face at the moment you came to it for help. Dark is the default, not a rule: change the appearance in Settings and the choice sticks.

How do I delete my data?

Meal events and grocery reminders can be deleted in Meals or in Apple's Calendar and Reminders apps. Deleting the app removes the recipes, roster and history stored on the device. Anything written to Apple Health is managed in the Health app, where entries can be deleted individually or entirely.
