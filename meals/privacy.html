Privacy Policy

Effective 20 August 2026

Meals collects no data. There is no account, no server run by the developer, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. What you plan and what you eat stay in your own Apple services and on your own device.

Where your information lives

  • Meal blocks are real events in your own Calendar, and groceries are real reminders in your own Reminders. They sync between your devices through your iCloud account, the same way your other calendars and lists do.
  • Recipes, textures, the safe-food roster and meal history stay on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Nutrition is written to Apple Health only if you turn that on. Meals only writes; it never reads your Health data. You can revoke the permission at any time in the Health app, and manage or delete the entries there.

What the developer receives

Nothing. No personal data, no usage data, no identifiers, no crash reports beyond what you may choose to share with Apple through iOS's own analytics settings.

Permissions the app asks for, and why

  • Calendar and Reminders (full access) — this is where meal blocks and the grocery list are stored. Meals writes only to the calendar and list it creates for itself.
  • Apple Health (optional, write only) — asked for only if you turn nutrition logging on.
  • Notifications (optional) — asked for only if you switch on meal warnings, the heads-up before a meal block. Off by default; the app never requests this permission before the toggle.

Scores, and the absence of them

Meals records what was eaten when you tell it to, and does nothing else with that. There are no calorie targets, no weight goals, no streaks, and no comparisons. The app has no opinion about your eating, by design.

Deleting your data

Meal events and grocery reminders can be deleted in Meals or in Apple's own apps. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device — recipes, the roster, and history. Health entries are yours to manage in the Health app.

Children

Meals is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour ever changes in a way that matters here, this page will change with it, and the date at the top will move.
