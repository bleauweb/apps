Apps by BleauWeb

We make small, calm apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac, built neurodivergent-friendly first. Each one gets a page here with the practical things: what it does, how to get help, and what happens to your data. The short answer to that last one is "almost nothing".

Why so quiet?

These apps are built for people who find interruption expensive. NeuRoutine and Wiederfind never send a notification at all; Meals can give a heads-up before dinner, but keeps that off until you ask. None of them have streaks, badges, scores, or an account to create, and what you make stays in your own Apple services, where we cannot read it.

The mission behind all of that is on the About page. Questions are always welcome: support@bleauweb.org.

There are more on the way.
