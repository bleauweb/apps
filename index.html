Why so quiet?

These apps are built for people who find interruption expensive. None of them send notifications — they never even ask for permission to. There are no streaks, no badges, no scores, and no account to create: everything you make lives in your own Apple services, synced through your own private iCloud, unreadable to us.

Questions about any of them, at any size: support@bleauweb.org.
