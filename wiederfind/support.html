Questions, answered

Where is my data stored?

On your device, and — when automatic iCloud sync is on — in the private iCloud attached to your Apple Account, which only your devices can read. Turn sync off in Settings → iCloud and everything simply stays on the device; Sync Now runs one sync by hand whenever you choose. There is no Wiederfind server and no account to create. The full story is the privacy policy.

Does Wiederfind send notifications?

No — never, and on purpose. No reminders to review, no streak warnings, no badges, and it never asks for notification permission. Reviews are ready when you open the app, and the app is silent when you don't.

Why are there only two answer buttons?

Each card asks one question: did you know it? Again if not, Good if you did — one less decision on every card. If you want finer control, Settings → Review turns on Hard and Easy too; the schedule works the same either way. Each button can also show when the card will come back (for example "6 days") before you press it, so nothing is a surprise.

How does the schedule decide when a card comes back?

Wiederfind uses FSRS-5, a modern spaced-repetition scheduler. The one dial worth knowing is Settings → Review → Target retention: how sure the schedule should be that you still remember a card when it returns. 90% is the usual balance — higher means more frequent reviews, lower means fewer.

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing breaks, because nothing is counting. There are no streaks and no timers; due cards simply wait, and the schedule takes the gap into account the next time you review. A session cap (Settings → Review) can keep any single sitting from growing past what you want.

What courses are included?

Complete German A1 and A2 courses, aligned with the Goethe-Zertifikat syllabus and reviewed against the official word lists. They install as lessons — a path of instruction pages, practice checks and decks, released at your pace. You can also build your own decks, or receive lessons other people share.

Why does the speech sound robotic, and how do I fix it?

Speech uses the voices installed on your device, and the basic ones do sound robotic. Download an Enhanced or Premium voice in iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices and it appears in Wiederfind straight away — Settings → Speech lets you pick and try voices per language. Sound only ever plays when you press a speaker button, and "Never make sound" silences the app entirely.

Someone shared a lesson with me — how does that work?

Sharing travels through iCloud, between your accounts — never through us. Nothing arrives without your acceptance, and anyone can be blocked in Settings → About. Pictures in shared lessons are checked on your device with Apple's Sensitive Content Warning when you have it on; a flagged picture is blurred and left out if you copy the lesson. If someone shares something that should not have been shared, write to us.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → iCloud → Reset the app offers the two honest choices: erase this device only (iCloud data survives and can sync back), or erase this device and iCloud — everywhere, permanently. Deleting the app removes everything on the device itself.
