Learning that stays calm

Wiederfind is a spaced-repetition app designed neurodivergent-friendly first: predictable screens, plain words, no timers, no streaks — and it never notifies.

The name is German — wiederfinden, "to find again". Retrieval practice is not putting knowledge in; it is coming upon what is already there, which is what a review is.

Lessons at your pace

A course is a path: instruction pages, practice checks and decks of cards, released at a pace the learner can keep up with — never all at once, never as a wall. Ships with complete German A1 and A2 courses aligned with the Goethe-Zertifikat syllabus, and new courses are plain files anyone can author.

An honest scheduler

Reviews use FSRS-5, and each card asks one question: did you know it? Two buttons — Again and Good — one decision per card, and each button can say when the card will come back before you press it, so nothing is a surprise.

Nothing counts your days

No timers, no streaks, no notifications — it never even asks for permission to notify. Sound only ever plays when you press a speaker button, with a single switch that makes the app completely silent. Learning happens when you open the app, and the app is silent when you don't.

The rest of the argument

Your data stays yours

Decks, cards and review history live on your device and, if you turn sync on, in your own private iCloud — which no one else can read. No account, no advertising, no analytics.

Sharing, carefully

People can share decks and lessons with you through iCloud. Nothing arrives without your say, anyone can be blocked, and pictures in shared lessons are checked on your device with Apple's Sensitive Content Warning when you have it switched on.

Speaks only when asked

Speech uses the voices installed on your device and never plays on its own. Choose a voice per language, try each one before choosing, and slow the speaking speed while you are learning sounds.

Calm is a feature

Predictable screens, plain language under every setting, and motion that follows your Reduce Motion preference. The calm is in the restraint, not the colour scheme.
