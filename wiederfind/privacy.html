Privacy Policy

Effective 20 August 2026

Wiederfind collects no data. There is no account, no server run by the developer, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Your decks, cards, lessons and review history stay on your device and, if you turn iCloud sync on, in your own private iCloud.

Where your information lives

  • On your device. Everything works with sync off, and with no network at all.
  • In your private iCloud, if you choose. With automatic sync on, your decks, cards and review history are kept the same on every device signed in to your iCloud account, through the private iCloud database attached to your Apple Account. Only your devices can read it. The developer cannot see it, query it, or recover it.

What the developer receives

Nothing. No personal data, no usage data, no identifiers, no crash reports beyond what you may choose to share with Apple through iOS's own analytics settings.

Sharing between people

Decks and lessons other people share with you travel through iCloud, between your accounts — never through the developer. Nothing arrives without your acceptance, and you can block anyone. Pictures in lessons other people share are checked on your device with Apple's Sensitive Content Warning, when that is turned on in Settings → Privacy & Security: a flagged picture is blurred, and left out when you copy the lesson. Nothing is sent anywhere for that check — it happens on the device.

Permissions

Wiederfind asks for none. It sends no notifications and never requests permission to notify. Speech uses the voices already installed on your device and plays only when you press a speaker button. If you add a picture to a card, Apple's photo picker hands the app only the picture you chose — the app never sees your library.

Deleting your data

Settings → iCloud → Reset the app offers both honest choices: erase this device only, or erase this device and your iCloud data — everywhere, permanently. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device itself.

Children

Wiederfind is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour ever changes in a way that matters here, this page will change with it, and the date at the top will move.
