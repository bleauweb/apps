A planner that stays calm

NeuRoutine is a low-friction planner for iPhone and iPad, designed neurodivergent-friendly first: one thing at a time, energy on an honest scale, and no interruptions — ever.

Today opens on the single thing that is up now, with one Done button and a quiet "then:" underneath. The full month exists, but it is never the front door.

Energy, not just time

Anything can carry an energy number — 0, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8 or 13 — and every day has the same fixed budget of 13. The meter reports what the day asks and what is left; it never tells you what to do about it. A day that asks for more than 13 says so in a sentence: a fact about the day, not about you.

The Energy tab looks backwards, too — which days were heavy, which weekday keeps being the heaviest — so patterns become visible without anyone scoring you.

Your data stays yours

Entries are real events and reminders in your own Calendar and Reminders — usable in Apple's apps, on every device you already have. The details Apple's apps have no place for sync through your private iCloud. No account, no server, nothing collected.

The privacy policy is one page, because there is almost nothing to say.

The rest of the argument

Big things break down

Any entry can hold small steps, and the focus card always names the next one. Capture asks exactly two things — what it is, and what it will cost — because every extra decision is a reason not to write the thing down at all.

It never interrupts

No notifications, no badges, no nudges about what is still undone — it never even asks for permission to notify. The app shows you your day when you open it, and is silent when you don't.

Predictable on purpose

Weeks always start on Monday, the month grid never changes shape, one colour carries one meaning, and a day can be dragged into your order — with the one rule that the clock never runs backwards.

At home on the platform

An On-deck widget with its own Done button, Lock Screen rings for progress and energy, a Control Centre capture button, and Siri shortcuts for adding, completing and asking how the day is going.
