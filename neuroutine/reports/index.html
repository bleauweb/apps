What this app decided, and whether it was right.

NeuRoutine is built on a set of written rules — about what it will show, what it will not say, and what it refuses to decide on anybody's behalf. Every so often those rules get read back against the published research on executive dysfunction, and the results are published here whether they were flattering or not.

  • The Rule Ledger

    22 August 2026

    All sixty-five of the app's design rules, audited one by one. Fifty-one held. Two worked against the app, and both were fixed the same day. The larger finding was that the rules are made entirely of restraint — which is very good at stopping the wrong thing being added, and completely silent on the right thing being missing.
