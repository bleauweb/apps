NeuRoutine — Privacy Policy

Effective 19 August 2026

NeuRoutine collects no data. There is no account, no server run by the developer, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Everything you plan stays in your own Apple Calendar, your own Apple Reminders, and your own private iCloud.

Where your information lives

  • Your entries — titles, notes, days, times, repeats — are stored as real events and reminders in your own Calendar and Reminders, exactly as if you had made them in Apple's apps. They sync between your devices through your iCloud account, the same way your other calendars do.
  • The details Apple's apps have no place for — an entry's energy estimate, its steps, and which days a repeat was completed — are stored on your device and synced through the private iCloud database attached to your Apple Account. Only your devices can read it. The developer cannot see it, query it, or recover it.
  • Nothing else is stored anywhere. The app makes no network connections except iCloud sync performed by Apple's own frameworks.

What the developer receives

Nothing. No personal data, no usage data, no identifiers, no crash reports beyond what you may choose to share with Apple through iOS's own analytics settings.

Permissions the app asks for, and why

  • Calendar and Reminders (full access) — this is the app's storage. NeuRoutine reads the calendars you choose in Settings to show your day, and writes only to its own calendar and its own list.

That is the whole list. NeuRoutine sends no notifications — it never asks for notification permission, never alerts, never badges, and never interrupts. It shows you your day when you open it, and is silent when you don't.

Sharing with third parties

There are none. NeuRoutine contains no third-party code, no SDKs, no advertising frameworks, and shares nothing with anyone.

Deleting your data

Your entries are yours: delete them in NeuRoutine, or in Apple's Calendar and Reminders apps, and they are gone. The synced details (energy, steps, completions) live in your iCloud account and can be removed by deleting the app's iCloud data in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device itself.

Children

NeuRoutine is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour ever changes in a way that matters here, this page will change with it, and the date at the top will move. Given what the app is — a planner that deliberately stores nothing outside your own Apple services — changes are expected to be rare.
